How much does the LIFEPAK 15 weigh?
The basic configuration of the LIFEPAK 15 with a new roll of paper and two batteries installed weighs 17.5 pounds. The fully featured configuration with a new roll of paper and two batteries installed weighs 18.5 pounds.
What are the dimensions of the LIFEPAK 15 monitor/defibrillator?
Height: 12.5 inches
Width: 15.8 inches
Depth: 9.1 inches
What are the six main operating modes on the LIFEPAK 15?
[[Sans]AED mode] - for automated ECG analysis and a prompted treatment protocol for patients in cardiac arrest
[[Sans]Manual mode] - for performing manual defibrillation, synchronized cardioversion, noninvasive pacing and ECG and vital sign monitoring
[[Sans]Archive mode] - for accessing stored patient information
[[Sans]Setup mode] - for changing default settings of the operation functions
[[Sans]Service mode] - for authorized personnel to perform diagnostic tests and calibrations
[[Sans]Demo mode] - for simulated waveforms and trend graphs for demonstration purposes
What are the display specifications on the LIFEPAK 15?
[[Sans]Size (active viewing area)] - 8.4 inches diagonal; 6.7 inches wide x 5.0 inches high
[[Sans]Resolution] - 640 dot x 480 dot color backlit LCD
[[Sans]User selectable display mode] - Users can choose between full color or SunVue display high contrast
[[Sans]Waveform display sweep speed] - 25 mm/second for ECG, SpO2, and IP; 12.5 mm/sec for CO2
Up to three waveforms may be displayed at the same time.
What alarms are included in the LIFEPAK 15?
[[Sans]Quick set] - activates alarm for all active vital signs
[[Sans]VF/VT alarm] - activates continuous (CPSS) monitoring in manual mode
[[Sans]No breath alarm] - occurs when 30 seconds has elapsed since the last detected respiration
[[Sans]Heart rate alarm limit range] - upper: 100-250 bpm; lower: 30-150 bpm
What type of battery is used in the LIFEPAK 15?
The LIFEPAK 15 uses a lithium-ion battery. Each battery has a fuel gauge that indicates its approximate charge.
Can I send transmissions while streaming is active?
Yes, the streaming will temporarily pause as the transmit is processing.
Can I provide therapy while streaming is active?
Yes, full monitoring and therapy abilities are available while streaming is active.
