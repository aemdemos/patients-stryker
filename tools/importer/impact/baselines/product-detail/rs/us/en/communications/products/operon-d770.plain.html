Operon D770

The Operon D770 offers an 18.3” wide integrated x-ray channel designed to eliminate the need for separate x-ray tops. The D770 maintains a 1,000lb weight capacity in any articulation in normal orientation.

With a focus on safety, the D770 provides your OR staff peace of mind with a manual backup system with foot pump and emergency keypad located on the column.
