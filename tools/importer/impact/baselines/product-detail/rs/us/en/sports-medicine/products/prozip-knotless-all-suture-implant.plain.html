[[Gold,2.5×]ProZip]
[[Large]Knotless All-Suture Implant]

ProZip is a knotless all-suture implant indicated for soft tissue approximation. It is designed to eliminate knot stacks through a simple and reproducible technique. It can be used in arthroscopic and open procedures.
