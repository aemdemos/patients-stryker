DOES THE POWER-PRO 2 COT WEIGH LESS THAN a COMPARABLE POWER-PRO XT CONFIGURATION?
Yes. The Power-PRO 2 cot is 8.4% lighter than a comparable Power-PRO XT configuration.
How much weight can the Power-PRO 2 support?
Stryker's Power-PRO 2 powered ambulance cot can support and transport a maximum weight of 700 lb (318 kg).
What standards does Power-PRO 2 meet?
The Power-PRO 2 has been dynamically crash tested to meet SAE J3027, BS EN 1789 and AS/NZS 4535 when used with Power-LOAD or Performance-LOAD fasteners, Stryker's X Restraints and Universal Floorplate. The Power-PRO 2 also meets IEC 60601-1 tip stability guidelines.
How often do I need to replace the battery in my Power-PRO 2?
The lithium-ion battery has a two-year expected service life under normal use conditions. The battery charger has a seven-year expected service life under normal use conditions.
Does Stryker offer training on Power-PRO 2?
Stryker sales representatives deliver hands-on training for Power-PRO 2 purchases.
Which fasteners are compatible with Power-PRO 2?
Power-PRO 2 is compatible with Stryker's Power-LOAD powered cot fastener, Performance-LOAD manual cot fastener, mass casualty fastener, wall mount fastener and floor mount fastener.
How do I implement Smart Equipment Management (SEM)?
Our implementation team can connect your cots to the LIFENET Portal. Contact your Stryker sales representative for more information.
What’s new in Power-PRO 2 compared with Power-PRO XT?
Power-PRO 2 introduces several enhancements, including bumper detection technology, a three-position foot section, transport height indicator, extended transport handle and Smart Equipment Management (SEM).
