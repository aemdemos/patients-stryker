EHR Connectivity

A solution that allows bi-directional communication with your EHR

The EHR connectivity solution serves as the bridge between your surgical devices, media management system, and hospital information systems—like your EHR and PACS. It transfers case data, images, and videos from devices such as the Connected OR into the patient’s record or Stryker's Surgical Media Management solution, without manual uploads or lost files. This connectivity software provides an integrated, secure, and reliable data management solution.
